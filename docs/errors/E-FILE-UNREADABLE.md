# E-FILE-UNREADABLE

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 1, File

## What it means

The workbook can't be opened; it may be damaged.

## How to fix it

Open it in Excel, save it again as .xlsx and upload the new file.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports:

> The workbook can't be opened; it may be damaged.
>
> → Open it in Excel, save it again as .xlsx and upload the new file.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
