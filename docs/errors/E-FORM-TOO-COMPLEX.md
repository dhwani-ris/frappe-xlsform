# E-FORM-TOO-COMPLEX

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 2, Structure

## What it means

The form couldn't be checked completely: it is nested too deeply for the ‹stage› checks.

## How to fix it

Use fewer groups inside groups and shorter formulas, then upload again.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey`:

> The form couldn't be checked completely: it is nested too deeply for the structure checks.
>
> → Use fewer groups inside groups and shorter formulas, then upload again.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
