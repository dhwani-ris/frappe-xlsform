# E-EXPR-TOO-LONG

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

The formula is ‹length› characters long; the limit is ‹limit›.

## How to fix it

Split it into calculate questions.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey!D3`:

> survey!D3: The formula is 59 characters long; the limit is 20.
>
> → Split it into calculate questions.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
