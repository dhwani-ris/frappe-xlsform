# E-LIST-TOO-LARGE

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

Choice list "‹list›" has ‹count› choices; the limit is ‹limit›.

## How to fix it

Split it with a cascading filter (district, then village), or ask your administrator about larger lists.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `choices`:

> Choice list "yn" has 2 choices; the limit is 1.
>
> → Split it with a cascading filter (district, then village), or ask your administrator about larger lists.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
