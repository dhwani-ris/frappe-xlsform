# E-SHEET-MISSING

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 1, File

## What it means

The workbook has no "‹sheet_name›" sheet.

## How to fix it

Rename your ‹purpose› sheet to "‹sheet_name›" (lower case).

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey`:

> The workbook has no "survey" sheet.
>
> → Rename your questions sheet to "survey" (lower case).

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
