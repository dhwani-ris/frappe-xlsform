# E-REF-SELF

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

The ‹column› of ‹name› refers to its own name; use . for "this answer".

## How to fix it

Replace ${‹name›} with .

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey!D2`:

> survey!D2: The constraint of age refers to its own name; use . for "this answer".
>
> → Replace ${age} with .

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
