# W-WEB-FILTER

**Warning: publish after ticking “I understand”** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

On the web form, ‹name› will list all choices of "‹list›"; answers that don't match the filter are rejected when saved.

## How to fix it

No change needed; the filter works on Desk and mForm.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey!D3`:

> survey!D3: On the web form, v will list all choices of "v"; answers that don't match the filter are rejected when saved.
>
> → No change needed; the filter works on Desk and mForm.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
