# E-NAME-DUPLICATE

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 2, Structure

## What it means

The name ‹name› is used in rows ‹rows›.

## How to fix it

Rename one, e.g. ‹example›.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey!B3`:

> survey!B3: The name age is used in rows 2 and 3.
>
> → Rename one, e.g. age_2.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
