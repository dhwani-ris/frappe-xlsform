# E-ROW-BUDGET

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 4, Frappe limits

## What it means

The form has too many short-answer questions outside repeats to store in one record (‹bytes› of ‹limit› bytes).

## How to fix it

Move some into a repeat, or shorten long choice names.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports:

> The form has too many short-answer questions outside repeats to store in one record (7,092 of 5,000 bytes).
>
> → Move some into a repeat, or shorten long choice names.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
