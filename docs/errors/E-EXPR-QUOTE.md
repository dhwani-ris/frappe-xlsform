# E-EXPR-QUOTE

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

The formula uses curly quotes (‘ ’ or “ ”), which formulas don't accept.

## How to fix it

Replace them with straight quotes (' or ").

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey!D3`:

> survey!D3: The formula uses curly quotes (‘ ’ or “ ”), which formulas don't accept.
>
> → Replace them with straight quotes (' or ").

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
