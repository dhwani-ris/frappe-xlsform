# W-INSTANCE-NAME

**Warning: publish after ticking “I understand”** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

instance_name can only combine questions and fixed text with concat(); it will be ignored.

## How to fix it

Example: concat(${village}, '-', ${hh_head})

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `settings!B2`:

> settings!B2: instance_name can only combine questions and fixed text with concat(); it will be ignored.
>
> → Example: concat(${village}, '-', ${hh_head})

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
