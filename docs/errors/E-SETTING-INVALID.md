# E-SETTING-INVALID

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

The setting ‹setting› = "‹value›" isn't valid: ‹reason›.

## How to fix it

Use lower-case letters, numbers and _, e.g. household_survey.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `settings!A2`:

> settings!A2: The setting form_id = "???" isn't valid: it has no letters or numbers to build a form ID from.
>
> → Use lower-case letters, numbers and _, e.g. household_survey.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
