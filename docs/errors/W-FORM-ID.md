# W-FORM-ID

**Warning: publish after ticking “I understand”** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

The form ID "‹value›" will be used as ‹fixed›, because form IDs may only use a-z, 0-9 and _.

## How to fix it

Change form_id to ‹fixed› in the settings sheet to keep them the same.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `settings!A2`:

> settings!A2: The form ID "Household Survey" will be used as household_survey, because form IDs may only use a-z, 0-9 and _.
>
> → Change form_id to household_survey in the settings sheet to keep them the same.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
