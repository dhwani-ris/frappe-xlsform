# E-COL-MISSING

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 2, Structure

## What it means

The ‹sheet_name› sheet has no "‹column›" column.

## How to fix it

Add a column named ‹column› in row 1.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey`:

> The survey sheet has no "name" column.
>
> → Add a column named name in row 1.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
