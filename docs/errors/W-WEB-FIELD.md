# W-WEB-FIELD

**Warning: publish after ticking “I understand”** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

‹name› (‹type›) can't be shown on the web form; it can be answered on Desk and mForm.

## How to fix it

No change needed, or untick Allow Web Form on the form.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey!A2`:

> survey!A2: gps (geopoint) can't be shown on the web form; it can be answered on Desk and mForm.
>
> → No change needed, or untick Allow Web Form on the form.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
