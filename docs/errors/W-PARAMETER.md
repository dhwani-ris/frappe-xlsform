# W-PARAMETER

**Warning: publish after ticking “I understand”** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

Parameter "‹parameter›" (row ‹row›) isn't supported and will be ignored.

## How to fix it

Remove it, or keep it for use in ODK.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey!D2`:

> survey!D2: Parameter "max-pixels" (row 2) isn't supported and will be ignored.
>
> → Remove it, or keep it for use in ODK.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
