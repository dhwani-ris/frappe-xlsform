# E-LIMIT-QUESTIONS

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 4, Frappe limits

## What it means

The form has ‹count› questions; the limit is ‹limit›.

## How to fix it

Split it into two forms, or ask your administrator to raise the limit.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports:

> The form has 2 questions; the limit is 1.
>
> → Split it into two forms, or ask your administrator to raise the limit.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
