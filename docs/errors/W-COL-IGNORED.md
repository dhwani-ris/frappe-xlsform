# W-COL-IGNORED

**Warning: publish after ticking “I understand”** · stage 2, Structure

## What it means

Column "‹column›" isn't supported yet and will be ignored.

## How to fix it

Remove it, or keep it for use in ODK.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey!D1`:

> survey!D1: Column "media::image" isn't supported yet and will be ignored.
>
> → Remove it, or keep it for use in ODK.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
