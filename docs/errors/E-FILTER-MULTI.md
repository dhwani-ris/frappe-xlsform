# E-FILTER-MULTI

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

choice_filter on select_multiple questions isn't supported yet (row ‹row›).

## How to fix it

Use select_one, or remove the filter.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey!D3`:

> survey!D3: choice_filter on select_multiple questions isn't supported yet (row 3).
>
> → Use select_one, or remove the filter.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
