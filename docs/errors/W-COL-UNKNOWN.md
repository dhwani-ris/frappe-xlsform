# W-COL-UNKNOWN

**Warning: publish after ticking “I understand”** · stage 2, Structure

## What it means

Column "‹column›" isn't recognised and will be ignored.

## How to fix it

Did you mean "label"?

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey!C1`:

> survey!C1: Column "lable" isn't recognised and will be ignored.
>
> → Did you mean "label"?

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
