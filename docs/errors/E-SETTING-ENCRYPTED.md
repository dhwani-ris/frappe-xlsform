# E-SETTING-ENCRYPTED

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

Encrypted forms (public_key) aren't supported.

## How to fix it

Remove the public_key setting.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `settings!B2`:

> settings!B2: Encrypted forms (public_key) aren't supported.
>
> → Remove the public_key setting.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
