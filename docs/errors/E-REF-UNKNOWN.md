# E-REF-UNKNOWN

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

The formula refers to ${‹name›}, which isn't a question in this form.

## How to fix it

Did you mean ${hh_size}?

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey!D3`:

> survey!D3: The formula refers to ${hh_sise}, which isn't a question in this form.
>
> → Did you mean ${hh_size}?

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
