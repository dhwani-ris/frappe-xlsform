# E-REPEAT-EMPTY

**Error: blocks publishing** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

Repeat ‹name› (row ‹row›) has no questions.

## How to fix it

Add questions between begin_repeat and end_repeat, or remove the repeat.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey!A3`:

> survey!A3: Repeat m (row 3) has no questions.
>
> → Add questions between begin_repeat and end_repeat, or remove the repeat.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
