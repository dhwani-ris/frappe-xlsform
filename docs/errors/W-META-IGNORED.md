# W-META-IGNORED

**Warning: publish after ticking “I understand”** · stage 3, Meaning

## What it means

"‹type›" (row ‹row›) can't be collected in this app and will be ignored.

## How to fix it

Remove the row, or keep it for use in ODK.

## Example

An upload that triggers it reports at `survey!A2`:

> survey!A2: "deviceid" (row 2) can't be collected in this app and will be ignored.
>
> → Remove the row, or keep it for use in ODK.

Words in ‹angle quotes› above are filled in with your form's own names, rows and values.
